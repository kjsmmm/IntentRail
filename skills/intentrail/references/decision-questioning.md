# Minimal necessary questions

Ask only when all conditions hold:

```text
information is genuinely missing
AND different answers materially change the result
AND no safe reversible default permits progress
```

Ask one ordinary blocking question per turn. Select the question with the highest information gain. Give two or three concrete options, explain the impact, and recommend one when evidence supports it.

Record a postponed decision as `DEFER`; do not repeat it until the relevant action becomes material. Silence never confirms a precedent, an assumption, or a high-risk choice.
